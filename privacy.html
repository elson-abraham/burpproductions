Who we are

This policy belongs to Burp Productions, a film and brand studio based in Bengaluru, India, reachable at burpproductions.com. It explains what happens to your information when you visit our site, send us a brief, or get in touch about a project.

00:01

Information we collect

When you use our contact form to send a brief or enquiry, we collect the details you provide — typically your name, email address, and the information about your project. We also log your IP address and browser data automatically, which helps us keep the site secure and filter spam submissions.

If our site ever accepts public comments, the same principle applies: we store what you submit along with your IP address and browser user agent for spam detection.

00:02

Media and uploads

Where the site allows image uploads, avoid files with embedded location data (EXIF GPS). Anyone who downloads an image from the site can extract that location data, so we recommend stripping it before sharing.

00:03

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to keep the site running smoothly:

  • Session cookies that remember your preferences while you browse.
  • If the site includes a login area, authentication cookies that persist for up to two weeks with "Remember Me" selected, and are removed on logout.
  • Basic analytics cookies to understand how visitors move through the site — no third-party ad tracking.
00:04

Embedded content

Our reel and case studies may embed video and content from platforms like YouTube or Vimeo. Embedded content behaves as if you visited that platform directly — it may set its own cookies and collect data about your interaction, particularly if you're logged into that platform at the time.

00:05

Who we share data with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only where necessary — for example, with email and hosting providers who help run the site, and with spam-filtering services that check submitted comments or form entries. Each of these partners is bound to protect your data and use it only to provide the service we've engaged them for.

00:06

How long we keep it

Enquiries and project briefs are kept for as long as needed to respond to you and, where a project goes ahead, for the duration of our working relationship plus a reasonable period afterward for our records. Comments, if enabled, are retained indefinitely so we can recognise return visitors, unless you ask us to remove them.

00:07

Your rights

You can ask us at any time for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or erase it. This doesn't cover information we're required to keep for administrative, legal, or security reasons. To make a request, reach out using the details below.
