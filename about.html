Behind the lens.
Beyond the ordinary.

Burp Productions is a Bengaluru-based film & brand studio. Eleven years in, we're still the same crew that shows up early, over-preps, and cares more about the cut than the credit.

Our story

started with one camera and a stubborn belief in good footage.

Burp Productions began in 2015 as a two-person crew shooting local ad spots on borrowed gear. No investors, no studio lot - just a conviction that most brand films were saying too much and showing too little.

Eleven years on, that conviction hasn't changed, even as the work has. We've grown into a full production house covering commercial films, social content, live events, music videos, and documentaries - but every project still runs through the same filter: does this shot earn its place in the cut?

Today the studio is built around a small group of directors, DOPs, editors, and producers who'd rather turn down a job than turn in something forgettable.

How we got here.

Eleven years & more, condensed into the moments that actually mattered.

2015

Two people, one camera

Burp Productions opens its doors in Bengaluru, shooting local commercial spots on limited equipment.

2018

First national campaign

A regional retail client's aftermovie gets picked up nationally, bringing the studio its first wave of brand clients outside Karnataka.

2021

In-house post & color

Editing, grading, and sound design move in-house, cutting turnaround time and giving the studio full control over the final frame.

2024

3D & AI visuals join the toolkit

A dedicated CGI and generative pipeline is added, letting the studio build worlds a camera alone can't reach.

2026

1000+ projects, 194 clients

The studio crosses a milestone reel: over a thousand projects delivered for brands, agencies, and founders across the country and Australia.
